CREATURES OF DREAMSCAPE

Dreamscape is home to many magical creatures. Some live peacefully alongside humans, while others protect ancient places or dangerous magical secrets.

MOONWOLVES

Moonwolves are intelligent wolves that live near the Moonlit Mountains. Their fur becomes silver under moonlight. They are highly sensitive to magical energy and can detect powerful magical objects from a great distance.

Moonwolves are especially connected to the Moon Crystal. Ancient guardians sometimes worked with them to protect the paths leading toward the mountains.

SKY SERPENTS

Sky Serpents are enormous flying creatures that live among the highest clouds. They can create powerful winds with their wings and are rarely seen by ordinary travelers.

According to old stories, Sky Serpents witnessed the Eclipse War from above and carried messages between distant kingdoms.

EMBER DRAGONS

Ember Dragons live inside volcanic regions. Their bodies naturally produce heat, allowing them to survive in extremely hot environments.

Although they are dangerous when threatened, Ember Dragons are not naturally evil. Some ancient mages believed their fire could be used to strengthen magical weapons.

MIST SPIRITS

Mist Spirits appear in forests and abandoned ruins. They are mysterious creatures made partly from magical mist.

They rarely communicate directly with humans. Instead, they sometimes guide travelers toward hidden locations or warn them about approaching danger.

SHADOW CROWS

Shadow Crows are small magical birds associated with the Shadow Realm. They can travel silently through darkness and are often used as messengers.

During the Eclipse War, Shadow Crows were used to carry information between enemy forces. Because of this history, many people still distrust them.

CRYSTAL FOXES

Crystal Foxes are rare creatures found near areas containing strong magical energy. Their fur contains tiny crystal-like formations that glow when magic is nearby.

They are curious but extremely difficult to approach. Legends claim that a Crystal Fox can lead a worthy traveler toward a hidden magical artifact.

THE GUARDIANS OF THE MOON

The Guardians of the Moon are not a single species but a legendary group of magical creatures believed to protect the Moon Crystal.

According to ancient records, they appeared after the Eclipse War and remained near the Moonlit Mountains.

Very few people know whether the Guardians still exist.

Some legends say they are waiting for the return of the Eclipse.
