DREAMSCAPE — MAGIC SYSTEM

Magic in Asteria

Magic is a natural force that exists throughout Asteria. It can be studied and practiced, but different people have different levels of magical ability. The four major forms of magic are Moon Magic, Fire Magic, Shadow Magic, and Healing Magic.

Moon Magic

Moon Magic is associated with the moon, light, protection, and magical spirits. It is strongest in areas that have a connection to the Moon Crystal.

Luna Vale is one of the strongest known users of Moon Magic. Her main defensive technique is Moonlight Shield, which creates a protective barrier. She can also communicate with Moon Spirits.

Moon Magic requires concentration. Using powerful spells for a long period can leave the user exhausted.

Fire Magic

Fire Magic is associated with heat, light, and controlled flames. It is commonly practiced in the Kingdom of Solara, where magical students can study it at the Solara Academy of Magic.

Aren Sol is a skilled Fire Mage. His strongest technique is Solar Burst, which creates a concentrated wave of heat and light.

Fire Magic is powerful but difficult to control. Strong emotions can make the magic unstable, especially for inexperienced users.

Shadow Magic

Shadow Magic allows users to manipulate darkness and shadows. It can be used for concealment, creating temporary barriers, and moving through dark environments without being easily noticed.

Kael Raven is one of the most skilled Shadow Magic users known in Asteria. He can use shadows to hide himself and create temporary shadow barriers.

Shadow Magic is particularly useful for exploration because it can help users avoid detection. However, advanced techniques require significant concentration.

Healing Magic

Healing Magic is used to restore energy and help injured people recover. Unlike Moon Magic, Fire Magic, and Shadow Magic, it focuses mainly on protection and recovery rather than combat.

Healing Magic is commonly taught to magical students who want to become healers. Powerful healing requires extensive training and cannot instantly solve every physical problem.

The Moon Crystal

The Moon Crystal is one of the most important magical artifacts in Asteria. It contains a large amount of magical energy and has a strong connection to Moon Magic.

The crystal is protected by Luna Vale. Ancient records suggest that its history is connected to the Eclipse War.

Many researchers believe that understanding the Moon Crystal could reveal important information about the origins of magic in Asteria.

Limits of Magic

Magic in Asteria is not unlimited. Every spell requires energy and concentration. Repeatedly using powerful spells can exhaust a magician.

Magical abilities also depend on training and natural affinity. A person who is highly skilled in one form of magic may not be equally skilled in another.

For this reason, successful magical teams often combine people with different abilities rather than relying on one type of magic.
