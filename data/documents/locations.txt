DREAMSCAPE — LOCATIONS

The Kingdom of Lunaris

The Kingdom of Lunaris is located in the northern part of Dreamscape. It is known for its connection to Moon Magic and its long history of studying the Moon Crystal.

The capital city of Lunaris contains the Royal Archive, where Mira Elian works as the royal archivist. The kingdom is generally peaceful, but its leaders remain concerned about the secrets surrounding the Moon Crystal.

The Kingdom of Solara

The Kingdom of Solara lies south of Lunaris. It is famous for its warm climate, bright landscapes, and strong tradition of Fire Magic.

The Solara Academy of Magic is located in the kingdom's capital. Young magicians from different regions can study Fire Magic and other magical disciplines at the academy.

Moonfall City

Moonfall City is an old city located near the border of Lunaris. It was built around an ancient crater that appeared during an unexplained magical event.

The city is an important destination for researchers studying Moon Magic. Several old buildings contain inscriptions that may provide clues about the history of the Moon Crystal.

The Whispering Forest

The Whispering Forest is a large forest between Lunaris and the northern mountains. Travelers often report hearing unusual sounds among the trees, especially at night.

The forest contains rare plants and several magical creatures. Moon Wolves are commonly found near its northern edge, while Shadow Ravens are often seen deeper inside the forest.

Because the paths can change unexpectedly, travelers are advised to explore the forest with an experienced guide.

The Crystal Caverns

The Crystal Caverns are a network of underground caves located beneath the eastern mountains.

The walls of the caverns contain naturally occurring crystals that glow when exposed to magical energy. Researchers believe that the crystals may have formed thousands of years ago.

Crystal Beasts are known to inhabit the deeper sections of the caverns. These creatures are usually peaceful but may become defensive when their territory is threatened.

The Forgotten Tower

The Forgotten Tower stands on an isolated hill between the Kingdom of Lunaris and the Whispering Forest.

The tower was abandoned many years ago. Its original purpose remains uncertain, although historical records suggest that it may have been used by magical researchers during the years before the Eclipse War.

Kael Raven has visited the tower several times because its hidden passages connect to old paths leading toward the northern regions.

The Royal Archive

The Royal Archive is located in the capital of Lunaris. It contains historical documents, maps, magical research, and records from the Eclipse War.

Mira Elian is responsible for maintaining the archive. Luna Vale frequently visits the archive to research information about the Moon Crystal and ancient Moon Magic.

The archive is one of the most important sources of historical knowledge in Dreamscape.
